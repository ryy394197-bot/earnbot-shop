# Why You Should Separate Pricing for SUPER and Ti Suffixes in Your Offerings

When it comes to selling or offering services in the tech and development space, one of the most common mistakes is to bundle pricing for different tiers or suffixes — like SUPER and Ti — into a single package. This can lead to confusion, missed revenue opportunities, and a poor customer experience. In this article, we’ll walk through why it's better to **separate pricing** for SUPER and Ti suffixes, and how to do it effectively.

---

## 1. **Clarify Value Perception**

Separating pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes allows you to **communicate the unique value** of each tier more clearly. Customers need to understand what they're paying for, and when you bundle different tiers into a single price point, it becomes harder for them to make an informed decision.

For example, if you're offering a service like GitHub repository management, the SUPER tier might include advanced analytics, while the Ti tier offers basic features. By separating the pricing, you can highlight the **additional features and benefits** of the SUPER tier, making it more appealing to customers who are willing to pay for more.

---

## 2. **Avoid Confusion and Improve Conversion**

One of the biggest advantages of separating pricing is **reducing confusion**. When customers see a single price for multiple tiers, they may hesitate or feel unsure about what they're getting. On the other hand, when you break down the pricing into clear, distinct tiers with their own feature sets and price points, customers can make **informed decisions** more quickly.

This also helps **improve conversion rates**. When pricing is clear and aligned with the perceived value of each tier, customers are more likely to choose the option that best fits their needs and budget.

---

## 3. **Maximize Revenue Potential**

By separating pricing, you open the door to **capturing more revenue** from different customer segments. Some customers may only need the basic Ti tier, while others are willing to pay for the premium SUPER tier. By offering both options separately, you can **target each group effectively** and maximize your overall revenue.

Additionally, you can use **pricing strategies** like tiered pricing, where customers can upgrade to a higher tier for additional features. This not only increases your average revenue per user but also encourages **customer loyalty** and long-term engagement.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Bundling Tiers into a Single Package**: As we've discussed, this can confuse customers and reduce conversion rates.
2. **Not Highlighting the Differences**: If you don't clearly communicate the benefits of each tier, customers may not understand the value of upgrading.
3. **Ignoring Customer Feedback**: Always listen to what your customers are saying. If they're confused or frustrated by your pricing structure, take that into account and adjust accordingly.

---

## How to Implement Separated Pricing Effectively

To make the most of separated pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes, follow these steps:

1. **Define the Features and Benefits of Each Tier**: Clearly outline what each tier offers, and make sure the differences are obvious.
2. **Use Clear and Simple Pricing**: Avoid jargon or complex pricing models. Keep it straightforward.
3. **Add Call-to-Actions (CTAs)**: Include clear CTAs on each tier’s landing page, such as “Upgrade to SUPER” or “Start with Ti.”
4. **Use Visuals and Comparisons**: Use tables or comparison charts to help customers see the differences between tiers at a glance.
5. **Test and Optimize**: Continuously test your pricing strategy and optimize based on customer behavior and feedback.
