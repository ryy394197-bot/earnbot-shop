Title: How to Set Up a Release Percentage for Your Supply Side Without Impacting Your Day-to-Day Usage

---

### Introduction

In the ever-evolving world of decentralized applications (DApps), supply-side monetization is becoming an increasingly popular way for developers and creators to earn revenue. However, setting up a release percentage can be tricky, as it needs to balance fair compensation for your efforts with ensuring you have enough resources during development. This article will guide you through the process of implementing a release percentage that supports both day-to-day operations and long-term growth.

### Step 1: Define Your Release Percentage

The first step is to decide on what percentage of the transaction fees (gas) you want to keep as a reward for users who use your DApp. This should be based on your development costs, marketing expenses, and desired profit margin. For example, if you estimate that 5% of transactions will go towards sustaining day-to-day operations, set this as your release percentage.

### Step 2: Implement Gas Fee Splitting

To ensure users don't notice a significant change in their transaction fees, you need to split the gas fee between your supply side and user-side. Most DApps use Ethereum or Binance Smart Chain (BSC), where developers can configure how much of the transaction is allocated to them.

For Ethereum:
- **EIP-1559**: This mechanism dynamically adjusts the base fee based on demand, which helps in maintaining a stable gas price.
- **Gas Limit Override**: Allow users to set their own gas limit, ensuring they pay exactly what they need while minimizing the impact of your release percentage.

### Step 3: Use a Gas Pool System

A gas pool system is an effective way to manage fees without significantly affecting daily usage. This involves setting up a smart contract that collects and distributes gas fees over time. Here’s how you can set it up:

1. **Create a Smart Contract**: Develop a smart contract that acts as the gas pool, collecting fees from transactions.
2. **Automate Distribution**: Set up periodic payouts to your supply side based on a predefined schedule (e.g., weekly or monthly).
3. **User Interface**: Provide users with an option to view their usage and the amount of gas they will be charged, ensuring transparency.

### Step 4: Monitor Usage and Adjustments

Regularly monitor the usage patterns and adjust your release percentage as needed. This might involve tweaking your smart contract logic, changing the gas pool distribution schedule, or even adjusting the overall release percentage based on market conditions or developer needs.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Overestimating Costs**: Always keep a realistic estimate of your development and operational expenses.
2. **Ignoring User Experience**: Ensure that users don't feel penalized by increased transaction fees; transparency is key.
3. **Insufficient Testing**: Thoroughly test the gas fee splitting mechanism to avoid unexpected issues.

### Conclusion

By carefully defining and implementing your release percentage, you can balance fair compensation with sustainable development. Using a gas pool system and keeping user experience in mind are crucial steps towards achieving this goal. Whether you’re developing on Ethereum or another blockchain network, these strategies can help ensure that both the supply side and users benefit from your DApp.
