**How to Set Your Release Percentage Without Affecting Your Daytime Usage as a Service Provider**

As a service provider in the world of online platforms, setting the right release percentage is crucial for maintaining a balance between monetization and usability. Whether you're running a SaaS, offering API access, or managing a marketplace, you want to ensure that your service remains accessible to users during peak hours while still generating revenue. In this article, we’ll explore practical steps to help you set your release percentage effectively without compromising your own usage during the day.

---

### 1. Understand Your Traffic Patterns

Before adjusting your release percentage, it’s essential to analyze your traffic data. Identify the times of day when your service experiences the highest demand. This could be during business hours, lunch breaks, or even late evenings, depending on your target audience.

By understanding these patterns, you can set your release percentage accordingly. For example, if your service is heavily used between 9 AM and 5 PM, you might want to allocate a higher percentage of capacity during these hours and reduce it during off-peak times.

---

### 2. Use Automated Scaling Tools

Many platforms offer automated scaling features that can help you manage your release percentage dynamically. These tools can automatically adjust the number of active instances or resources based on real-time demand.

For example, if you're using ComputeGate or similar services, you can set rules that increase the release percentage during high-traffic periods and decrease it when demand drops. This ensures that your service remains responsive during peak hours while minimizing costs during off-peak times.

---

### 3. Implement Priority Access for Yourself

One of the most effective ways to ensure that your own usage isn’t affected by the release percentage is to set up priority access. This means reserving a portion of your capacity specifically for your own use, regardless of the overall release percentage.

You can achieve this by configuring your platform to always allocate a certain number of resources to your account. This way, even if the release percentage is low, you’ll still have access to the resources you need during the day.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Ignoring Traffic Patterns:** Failing to analyze your traffic data can lead to inefficient resource allocation and potential service disruptions.
- **Overlooking Automation:** Relying solely on manual adjustments can be time-consuming and error-prone.
- **Not Reserving Priority Access:** Without a dedicated allocation for your own use, you risk being locked out of your service during peak hours.
