**Why You Should Separate Pricing for SUPER and Ti Suffixes in Your Offerings**

When it comes to monetizing your services or products, especially in the tech and freelance space, pricing strategy plays a crucial role. One often-overlooked detail is the distinction between pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes. While it might seem like a minor point, separating these two can significantly impact your revenue, client perception, and long-term growth. In this article, we’ll break down why this distinction matters and how you can implement it effectively.

---

### 1. **Understanding the Value Perception of SUPER vs. Ti**

SUPER and Ti are not just arbitrary labels—they represent different levels of service, performance, or features. Clients are often more willing to pay a premium for a product or service that is clearly labeled as “SUPER” because it signals higher quality, more robust features, or better support.

On the other hand, the Ti suffix might be perceived as a more affordable or entry-level option. By separating the pricing, you’re allowing clients to choose based on their budget and needs, which improves conversion rates and customer satisfaction.

**Actionable Step:** Clearly define what each suffix entails in your product description. Make sure your SUPER offering includes advanced features or support that justifies the higher price point.

---

### 2. **Avoid Confusion and Improve Transparency**

One of the biggest mistakes freelancers and service providers make is bundling all their offerings into a single price point. This can confuse clients and lead to hesitation in making a purchase. By separating the pricing for SUPER and Ti, you’re making your offerings more transparent.

Clients appreciate clarity. When they see two clear options—one for the premium SUPER and one for the more affordable Ti—they can make an informed decision without feeling pressured or confused.

**Actionable Step:** Use clear headings and bullet points to differentiate between the SUPER and Ti packages. Highlight the key features of each in your pricing table or landing page.

---

### 3. **Maximize Revenue Through Tiered Pricing**

Tiered pricing allows you to capture more value from your clients. While some may only be able to afford the Ti option, others are willing to pay for the SUPER tier. By offering both, you can cater to a wider audience and increase your overall revenue.

This strategy is particularly effective when you’re targeting both small businesses and larger enterprises. The Ti package can serve as an entry point, while the SUPER package can be upsold to clients who need more advanced features or support.

**Actionable Step:** Create a pricing page that clearly outlines the differences between the two tiers. Use a comparison table to highlight what’s included in each.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Merging Prices Without Justification:** If you don’t clearly explain why the SUPER option is more expensive, clients may feel it’s overpriced.
- **Overloading the Ti Package with Features:** This can devalue the SUPER tier and reduce the perceived difference between the two.
- **Failing to Update Pricing Across All Channels:** Ensure that your pricing is consistent across your website, GitHub, and any social media platforms you use.
