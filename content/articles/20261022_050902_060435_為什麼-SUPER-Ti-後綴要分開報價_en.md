**Why You Should Separate Pricing for SUPER and Ti Suffixes in Your Offerings**

In the world of software development and SaaS, pricing is one of the most critical decisions you can make. It not only affects your revenue but also shapes customer perception and long-term relationships. When it comes to offering services or products with different tiers—such as SUPER and Ti suffixes—it's essential to understand why separating their pricing is a smart move. In this article, we’ll explore the practical reasons behind this approach and how you can implement it effectively.

---

### 1. **Clarify Value Perception**

Separating the pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes helps customers understand the distinct value each tier offers. The SUPER tier is typically associated with advanced features, higher performance, or exclusive access, while the Ti tier may offer a more streamlined or cost-effective option. By clearly distinguishing their pricing, you make it easier for potential clients to choose the plan that best fits their needs and budget.

This clarity reduces confusion and increases the likelihood of a sale. Customers are more likely to commit when they can easily see what they’re paying for and what they’re getting in return.

---

### 2. **Avoid Pricing Confusion**

One of the most common mistakes in pricing is to bundle different tiers together or allow customers to mix and match features. This can lead to confusion and dissatisfaction. For example, a customer might pay for the SUPER tier but only use features from the Ti tier, leading to frustration and a sense of being overcharged.

By keeping the pricing for each suffix separate, you eliminate this ambiguity. Each tier becomes its own standalone product, making it easier for customers to understand what they are paying for and ensuring that they only pay for the features they need.

---

### 3. **Maximize Revenue Opportunities**

Separating pricing allows you to capture more revenue from different customer segments. Some customers may be willing to pay a premium for the SUPER tier, while others may prefer the more affordable Ti tier. By offering both tiers separately, you can cater to a wider audience and increase your overall revenue potential.

This approach also makes it easier to introduce new tiers or features in the future without disrupting the existing pricing structure. You can gradually introduce more advanced options without alienating your current customer base.

---

### 4. **Enhance Marketing and Sales Strategy**

When you separate pricing, you can tailor your marketing and sales messages more effectively. Each tier can have its own unique selling points, target audience, and promotional strategy. For example, you can highlight the advanced features of the SUPER tier in marketing materials aimed at enterprise clients, while promoting the affordability and simplicity of the Ti tier to small businesses or individual users.

This segmentation also allows you to create more targeted landing pages, sales funnels, and promotional campaigns that speak directly to the needs and interests of each customer segment.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Mixing pricing tiers:** Allowing customers to select features from different tiers can lead to confusion and dissatisfaction.
- **Not clearly defining value:** Failing to explain the differences between tiers can make it difficult for customers to choose the right option.
- **Overlooking customer feedback:** Not listening to customer feedback can lead to pricing strategies that don’t align with market demand.
