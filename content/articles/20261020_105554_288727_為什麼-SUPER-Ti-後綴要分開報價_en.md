**Why You Should Separate Pricing for SUPER and Ti Suffixes in Your Offerings**

When building your service or product offerings, especially in the tech or development space, it's easy to fall into the trap of lumping all your services into a single package. However, for those who are serious about maximizing their revenue and ensuring clarity for clients, it's essential to **separate pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes**. This approach not only helps in better monetization but also improves client satisfaction and long-term business growth.

---

### 1. **Clarity for Clients: Avoid Confusion**

One of the most common mistakes freelancers and service providers make is bundling all their offerings into a single, all-inclusive package. While this may seem convenient, it can lead to confusion for potential clients who may not know exactly what they're paying for.

By **separating pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes**, you give your clients a clear breakdown of what each tier includes. This transparency helps clients understand the value they're getting and makes it easier for them to choose the right option for their needs.

For example, if you're offering a custom website development service, you might have a **Ti** tier that includes basic features like a homepage and contact form, while the **SUPER** tier offers advanced features like e-commerce integration and custom animations. Separating these into distinct pricing tiers ensures that clients are not overpaying for features they don't need.

---

### 2. **Maximize Revenue Through Tiered Pricing**

Another benefit of separating pricing for SUPER and Ti suffixes is the potential to **increase your overall revenue**. When clients see the difference in features and value between the two tiers, they're more likely to choose the higher-priced option if it aligns with their needs.

This tiered approach allows you to **capture more value from clients who are willing to pay for premium features**, while still offering an affordable option for those with simpler requirements. It’s a win-win: you get more money for your services, and your clients get exactly what they need without paying for unnecessary extras.

---

### 3. **Better Client Retention and Upselling Opportunities**

When you separate pricing, you also open the door for **future upselling and client retention**. Clients who start with the Ti tier may be more likely to upgrade to the SUPER tier as their business grows and their needs become more complex.

This strategy not only helps you **retain clients over the long term** but also builds trust and loyalty. Clients appreciate the flexibility and the ability to scale their service as their needs evolve.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Bundling everything into one package**: This can lead to confusion and may not reflect the true value of your services.
- **Not clearly defining what each tier includes**: Vague descriptions can lead to dissatisfaction and disputes.
- **Failing to highlight the differences between tiers**: If clients don’t understand why one tier is more expensive than the other, they may not see the value in upgrading.
