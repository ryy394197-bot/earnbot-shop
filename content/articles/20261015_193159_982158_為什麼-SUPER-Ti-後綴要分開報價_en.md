**Why You Should Separate Pricing for SUPER and Ti Suffixes in Your Offerings**

When building your product or service offering, especially in the tech space, it's easy to get caught up in the excitement of bundling everything together. However, when it comes to services like SUPER and Ti, it's crucial to understand why separating their pricing can be more effective — and even necessary — for your business and your clients.

---

### 1. **Clarify Value Perception**

One of the biggest mistakes developers and service providers make is assuming that all clients see the same value in every feature. SUPER and Ti are not just different versions of the same product; they are distinct in their capabilities, performance, and use cases.

By separating pricing, you're giving your clients the clarity they need to make informed decisions. This approach can help avoid confusion and ensure that clients are paying for exactly what they need — not a bundled package that includes features they don’t require.

---

### 2. **Tailor Pricing to Specific Needs**

Different clients have different needs. Some may require the full power of Ti, while others may only need the basic features of SUPER. Offering a single bundled price might lead to underutilization of Ti or overpayment for SUPER.

Separating the pricing allows you to create more tailored pricing models. For example, you can offer a basic plan for SUPER at a lower price and a premium plan for Ti that includes advanced features. This flexibility can significantly increase your appeal to a wider range of clients.

---

### 3. **Avoid Confusion and Improve Negotiations**

Bundling SUPER and Ti into one package can create confusion, especially during sales negotiations. Clients may not understand the difference between the two, leading to misunderstandings and potential dissatisfaction later on.

By clearly separating the pricing and explaining the differences in performance, scalability, and use cases, you can make the sales process smoother and more transparent. This not only improves client satisfaction but also helps you avoid unnecessary disputes or returns.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Assuming All Clients Want the Same Features:** Not all clients need the full power of Ti. Some may only need the basic functionality of SUPER. Make sure to offer both options clearly.
  
- **Overlooking the Importance of Clarity:** A single bundled price might seem convenient, but it can lead to confusion and miscommunication. Always prioritize clarity over simplicity.

- **Not Highlighting the Differences:** It’s essential to clearly explain the differences between SUPER and Ti. This helps clients understand the value of each option and makes it easier for them to choose the right one for their needs.
