**How Suppliers Can Set Release % Without Affecting Their Own Usage During the Day**

In the world of decentralized computing, setting the right release percentage is crucial for both profitability and usability. As a supplier, you want to maximize your earnings without compromising your own ability to use the resources during peak hours. This article will guide you through practical steps to achieve this balance, ensuring your setup is both efficient and profitable.

---

### 1. Understand Your Usage Patterns

Before adjusting your release percentage, it's essential to understand your own usage patterns. Identify the times of day when you use the most resources. Are you a heavy user during the day, or do you primarily use the system during off-peak hours?

By tracking your usage, you can determine how much of your capacity you can afford to release without affecting your own needs. Tools like ComputeGate's analytics dashboard can help you monitor your usage in real-time, giving you the data you need to make informed decisions.

---

### 2. Set a Dynamic Release Schedule

Instead of setting a static release percentage, consider implementing a dynamic schedule that changes based on the time of day. For example, you might set a higher release percentage during off-peak hours and lower it during the day when you're more likely to use the resources.

This approach allows you to maximize your earnings during times when demand is high while still reserving enough capacity for your own needs during the day. Many platforms, including ComputeGate, support dynamic scheduling, making it easy to implement.

---

### 3. Use Priority Allocation

Another effective strategy is to use priority allocation. This means that your own usage is given priority over external requests. Even if your release percentage is high, your own tasks will be processed first, ensuring that you can still use the resources you need during the day.

Priority allocation can be set up through your platform's settings, and it's a feature that many decentralized computing platforms support. By leveraging this feature, you can maintain control over your resources while still benefiting from the income generated by releasing capacity.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Setting a static release percentage without considering your usage patterns.** This can lead to either underutilization of your resources or a poor user experience if you're not available when needed.
- **Not monitoring your usage in real-time.** Without real-time data, it's difficult to make adjustments that keep your setup both efficient and profitable.
- **Over-releasing capacity without setting up priority allocation.** This can result in your own tasks being delayed or even skipped, which can be frustrating and counterproductive.
