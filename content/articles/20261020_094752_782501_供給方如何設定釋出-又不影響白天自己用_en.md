**How Suppliers Can Set Release % Without Disrupting Daytime Usage**

In the world of decentralized infrastructure and shared resource platforms, managing the balance between availability and profitability is a delicate art. As a supplier on platforms like ComputeGate, you're often faced with the challenge of setting a release percentage that ensures your resources are monetized effectively while still being available for your own needs during the day. This article will guide you through practical steps to achieve this balance, while avoiding common pitfalls.

---

### 1. Understand Your Resource Usage Patterns

Before you can set a release percentage, you need to understand your own usage patterns. Are you a heavy user during the day, or do you only need the resource during off-peak hours? Tools like usage analytics or even simple time-tracking can help you identify when your own usage is highest. Once you know your peak times, you can set your release percentage accordingly.

For example, if you're a developer who uses your machine heavily during the day, you might want to set your release percentage lower during those hours and higher during the night when demand is lower. This ensures that you're still able to use the resource when you need it most, while still making it available to others when it's not in use.

---

### 2. Use Dynamic Allocation Tools

Many platforms now offer dynamic allocation features that allow you to set different release percentages based on time or demand. These tools can automatically adjust your release percentage based on real-time usage and demand. This is especially useful if you're not sure about your own usage patterns or if they change frequently.

By using dynamic allocation, you can ensure that your resource is always available when it's needed most, while still being released to others when it's not in use. This not only helps you maximize your earnings, but also ensures that your own usage is not disrupted.

---

### 3. Set a Minimum and Maximum Release Percentage

To avoid over-releasing your resource and still keeping it available for your own use, it's a good idea to set both a minimum and maximum release percentage. This way, you can ensure that your resource is always available for your own use during peak hours, while still being released to others during off-peak hours.

For example, you might set a minimum release percentage of 20% during the day and a maximum of 80% during the night. This ensures that your resource is always available for your own use during the day, while still being released to others when it's not in use.

---

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Over-releasing your resource**: Setting a high release percentage during peak hours can lead to your own usage being disrupted. It's important to set a minimum release percentage that ensures your own usage is not affected.
- **Not monitoring your usage patterns**: Failing to monitor your own usage patterns can lead to poor resource allocation decisions. Always track your usage and adjust your release percentage accordingly.
- **Ignoring dynamic allocation tools**: Many platforms offer dynamic allocation tools that can help you set your release percentage based on real-time usage and demand. Ignoring these tools can lead to suboptimal resource allocation.
